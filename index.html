We make films that pay attention to place, people, memory and time.

A boutique studio for Nepali stories.

नेपाली कथा, हाम्रो दृष्टि

We are a small, hands-on team. We develop scripts slowly, shoot on location, and stay with each film from first draft to final grade.

Our stories are rooted in Nepali culture, landscape, and lived experience — village courtyards and Kathmandu rooftops, migration and return, ritual and everyday work.

What we documentFeatures, documentaries and the people who make them.
What we publishFestival records, press notes and location references.
Who we work withDirectors, actors and craftspeople across Nepal.
Why the archive existsSo Nepali cinema is remembered as culture, not just content.
