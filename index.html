Chalachitra is a Kathmandu-based film production house.

[Scroll down]
0

We make films that pay attention to place, people, memory and time.

A boutique studio for Nepali stories.

नेपाली कथा, हाम्रो दृष्टि

We are a small, hands-on team. We develop scripts slowly, shoot on location, and stay with each film from first draft to final grade.

Our stories are rooted in Nepali culture, landscape, and lived experience — village courtyards and Kathmandu rooftops, migration and return, ritual and everyday work. We treat every film as a single work with a singular vision.

OUR VALUES

Our values

हाम्रा मूल्यहरू

We hold to the authenticity of Nepali stories, to hands-on craft from script to screen, and to audience-first storytelling — films made with care, for the people they belong to.

OUR VISION

Our vision

हाम्रो दृष्टि

3.1

Story integrity

Scripts rooted in Nepali context, culture, and lived truth. One film, one singular cinematic vision — never diluted.

3.2

Innovation

Technology as a tool, the human touch driving creativity. We invest in research, workshops, and the festival circuit.

3.3

Enhanced experience

The emotional resonance and wellbeing of the audience come first. Cinema that enriches lives, long after the lights come up.
